# Brand guidelines

## Who we are

OruSchools runs a whole school (admissions, attendance, fees, transport and messages) and keeps working when the network doesn't. It replaces paper registers, scattered spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups with one calm system. Teachers, parents, drivers and office staff can all use it without training.

- **Purpose:** give every school the tools that only big schools used to have, without making anyone feel slow or left out.
- **Promise:** *Simple enough for everyone. Reliable when it matters.*
- **Tagline:** *Schools. People. Progress.* Use it under the logo, at the end of a presentation, or in a footer. Don't rewrite it.
- **Personality:** modern, trustworthy, academic, calm, approachable. We sound like a capable school administrator who is also kind, not like a tech startup and not like a government form.

## How we sound

| Audience | Sound like | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Principals and management | Clear, confident, factual | “3 classes have not sent attendance yet.” |
| Teachers | Brief, helpful, on their side | “Next class in 25 mins. Take attendance.” |
| Parents | Warm, reassuring, about their child | “Ananya is present today. 2 classes completed.” |
| Drivers | Short commands, big words | “Next stop: Greenwood Apartments. 5 min.” |
| Sales and marketing | Plain benefits, real numbers, no hype | “Attendance in 30 seconds, even offline.” |

Never say “revolutionary”, “AI-powered”, “seamless”, “synergy” or “cutting-edge”. Say what it does. Write for someone reading in their second language: short sentences, common words, one idea per sentence.

## The logo

The mark is the letter **O** for *Oru*. A ring with an opening, and a dot stepping out of it:

- the **ring** is the school: students, teachers and parents held together;
- the **opening** means the school is open to parents, never a closed office;
- the **dot** is the student, moving forward. It's the *Progress* in our tagline.

The wordmark *OruSchools* is set in **Poppins Bold** with tight spacing and converted to outlines. Its round, geometric **O** repeats the mark. Never retype the logo in a font; always use the supplied files.

### Colour of the logo

- **On white or light backgrounds:** mark in Oru Indigo `indigo-600`, wordmark in Ink `text` (#151838). This is the primary logo.
- **On Oru Indigo, dark backgrounds or photographs:** all white (`-white` files). Place it on a calm, dark area of the photo.
- **One colour only** (stamps, embroidery, newspaper, fax): all Ink (`-ink` files) or all Indigo (`-indigo` files).
- Never put the indigo mark on a coloured background other than white or `indigo-50`.

### Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo at least the **diameter of the dot**, which is about a quarter of the mark's height. No text, edges or other logos inside it. The supplied files already include this space.
- **Minimum size:** mark 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print. Horizontal logo 96 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that, use the mark alone.
- The favicon (`favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`) is a separate, heavier drawing for tiny sizes. Don't shrink the normal mark to 16 px when the favicon fits.

### Don't

1. Don't change the colours, or put the indigo mark on another colour.
2. Don't stretch, squash, rotate or skew it.
3. Don't close the ring or move the dot. The opening and the dot are the logo.
4. Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or 3D effects.
5. Don't retype the wordmark or change its font.
6. Don't put it on busy photos or patterns without a calm area behind it.
7. Don't place it inside a box, badge or circle you've made yourself. Use the supplied app icons for that.
8. Don't combine it with another logo except in the co-branding layouts below.

## Brand colours

The product palette lives under **Colour** in this system. For the brand:

- **Oru Indigo** (`indigo-600`, #4F46E5) is our colour. It should be the one colour people remember. Use it for the mark, key headlines in marketing and one main shape per layout.
- **Ink** (`text`, #151838) for words, **Indigo 50** (`indigo-50`) and **page grey** (`bg`) for calm backgrounds, **white** for space.
- In marketing, Oru Indigo should cover no more than about a third of any layout. The rest is white, grey and ink. Calm, not colourful.
- **App colours** (`app-erp`, `app-teacher`, `app-staff`, `app-parent`, `app-driver`) exist so people can tell the apps apart on a crowded phone screen. Use them **only** on app icons, store screenshots and app-specific marketing, never inside the product UI.

## Typography

- **Poppins** for the logo only (outlined in the files), plus marketing headlines: posters, social posts, presentation titles, website hero text. Use Bold for headlines and Medium for short sub-heads.
- **Noto Sans** for everything else: the product, body text in documents, emails, captions, and every language other than English. It covers Hindi, Urdu and other Indian scripts in the same voice.
- Never use more than these two. Never use Poppins for paragraphs, tables or product UI.

## App icons

All five icons share one construction. A full-bleed square in the app's colour, with the mark centred at 55% of the width. The phone applies its own rounded corners, so the files are square.

- Use the 1024 px PNGs for the App Store and Play Store. The SVGs are for any other size.
- The mark stays within the Android adaptive-icon safe zone, so nothing is cut off.
- Don't add text, badges or school logos to app icons. In white-label Mode B, the school supplies its own icon.

## Co-branding with schools (white-label)

**Mode A: Powered by OruSchools.** The app is still *OruSchools Teacher / Parent / …*, but inside it the school leads:

- The app header shows the **school's logo and name** (`cobrand-app-header.svg`). On the splash screen, the school is centred and **Powered by OruSchools** sits at the bottom (`cobrand-splash.svg`). The ERP sidebar works the same way (`cobrand-erp-sidebar.svg`).
- The school logo sits on a soft square (`surface-brand`) so crests of any shape line up. The grey shield in the files is only a placeholder.
- The **Powered by** lockup (`powered-by-oruschools.svg`) must stay visible on the splash screen and the login screen, at least 14 px mark height. Never put it above or bigger than the school's logo.
- A school may change only the primary colour (see Colour in the main guide). Our logo keeps its own colours.

**Mode B: Full white label.** The school's app name, icon and splash screen replace ours completely, and the OruSchools logo doesn't appear in the app. The words *Powered by OruSchools* stay, in plain text, on the About screen.

## Splash screens

Every app opens on its own colour with the white mark in the middle, so the screen continues straight on from the icon the person just tapped. The Driver app uses an Ink mark on yellow. The app name sits at the bottom so people know they opened the right app.

**Layout (all apps):** full-bleed `app-*` colour. The mark is 160 dp, centred on the screen. The app lockup (mark, OruSchools and app name) is 40 dp tall and 48 dp above the bottom edge. No other text, no loading spinner, no tips or adverts.

**Animation (1.3 s, once):**

| Time | What moves | Easing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 ms | Nothing. Frame 0 is the native launch screen, so the switch can't be seen. | |
| 0–170 ms | The dot tucks in to 80%. | ease-in-out |
| 100–900 ms | The whole mark turns once (360°). | cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1) |
| 900–1070 ms | The dot pops to 130%. | ease-in-out |
| 1070–1270 ms | The dot settles back to 100%. The app's first screen then fades in over 200 ms. | ease-in-out |

**Rules**

- Play it on a **cold start only**. A warm start goes straight into the app.
- Never hold the screen longer than needed. If the app is ready before 1.3 s, let the animation finish; if it is still loading after it ends, stay on the last frame. Don't loop it, and don't add a spinner. On weak networks, the app opens from cached data (the sync banner tells people the state).
- With *Reduce motion* or *Remove animations* on, show the static screen only.
- **White-label Mode A:** use the co-branded splash in Assets › Co-branding (school logo centred, Powered by OruSchools at the bottom), without animation.
- **Mode B:** the school supplies its own splash. Keep the same layout and timing.

**Building it:**

- **Flutter:** `flutter_native_splash` with `color` = the app colour, `image` = `splash-icon-white.png` (or `-ink` for Driver), `branding` = `splash-branding-<app>.png`, and the same values under `android_12`. Then show `splash-mark-<ink>.lottie.json` with the `lottie` package at 288 × 288 dp, centred on the same colour, and route on its completion.
- **Web ERP:** the same layout as an HTML loading screen on `app-erp`, using the same timing in CSS. The SplashScreen page in this system shows the reference animation.

## Imagery

- Real schools, real light: classrooms, corridors, buses, parents at the gate. Natural daylight, people actually doing something, children's faces only with school consent.
- Don't use staged stock photos (people pointing at laptops, graduation caps thrown in the air), 3D cartoon characters, or emoji used as decoration.
- Illustrations, if needed, are simple flat shapes built from the logo's ring, pill and circle, in Indigo tints. No gradients.
- Product screenshots always show realistic data: real-looking Indian names, ₹ amounts and Grade 10 A, not “Lorem ipsum” or “John Doe”.

## Social, email and documents

- **Profile picture:** `social-avatar-1080.png` (white mark on Oru Indigo). Platforms crop it to a circle; the mark is sized for that.
- **Page banner:** `social-banner-1500x500.png` (LinkedIn and X). **Link preview:** `og-image-1200x630.png` is the website's `og:image`.
- **Email signature:** `email-signature.html` is the live version. Paste it into Gmail or Outlook settings and change the name, role and contact line. The PNG and SVG are for previews only.
- **Letterhead:** `letterhead-a4.pdf` (A4). Replace the placeholder address and contact line before first use. Write letters in Noto Sans 10–11 pt, Ink.
- **Presentations:** white slides with the horizontal logo top-left on the title slide, and the mark bottom-right on the others. Titles in Poppins Bold, body in Noto Sans.
