# ADR-0018: Messaging and the school notice board

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-08), owner answers in chat to the questions D1 to D8 of `documentation/modules/messaging-and-notice-board.md` (D8 and the unanswered details take the recommendation, marked **default**).
- **Plan:** `documentation/modules/messaging-and-notice-board.md`. Tasks P5-3 and P5-8 in `documentation/phase5_plan.md`.
- **Builds on:** ADR-0001 (tenant isolation), ADR-0007 (roles; silos), ADR-0013 (notices in the cause's transaction), ADR-0014 (offline sync), ADR-0015 decision 2, ADR-0016 (comments: a different thing, in the open under a worksheet).

## Decisions
1. **Backend (D1):** messages go **through the gateway** and are stored in Data Connect. Nothing in front of the clients talks to a database.
2. **Office hours (D2): changed by the owner.** A message sent at any hour is **delivered at once** to the other person; the receiver may read and **reply whenever they choose**. There is no holding of notifications and no refusal outside office hours. This **amends** `documentation/guardrails.md` section 3 ("direct messaging operates strictly within Office Hours"), which is edited in the same change. What stays: **every message is logged to the central audit trail the System Admin can read**, and no unrestricted un-auditable messaging exists. Consequence for ADR-0016: the teacher's notice about a new comment (not held for office hours) is now consistent with this rule.
3. **Who may send one message to the whole class (D3):** the **class teacher** of that class; the **Principal and the Academic admin** may send to any class or grade. A subject teacher cannot broadcast (one-to-one only).
4. **Guardians (D4):** **a separate private conversation per parent account** for each student. Neither parent sees the other's messages.
5. **Notice board posters (D5):** the **Principal and the Academic admin**, and also the **Bursar for the Fees category** and the **transport manager for the Transport category**; each of the last two can post only in their own category.
6. **Languages (D6):** the sender writes **one text in one language**; screens around it are in the reader's chosen language. No per-language versions in version 1.
7. **Hiding and reporting (D7):** a sender may **hide** their own message for a short time (**default: 5 minutes**, like comments); a hidden message stays in the audit trail. A **Report** action on any message sends it to the **Principal**, who reviews reports.
8. **Naming (D8, default):** the table stays `Announcement`, extended with an audience (staff, parents, a grade, a class, all), category, pinned, publish and expiry dates; the parent-facing screen is called **Notice board**.
9. Unchanged from the plan: one way broadcasts (a parent replies privately about their own child, never into the broadcast); no phone numbers or emails shown to the other side; no attachments in version 1; no staff-to-staff messaging; no parent-to-parent chat; recipients of a broadcast are resolved on the server from the class's current students and their guardians; sending offline is saved on the phone and sent once.

## Consequences
- `P5-3a` (this ADR) is done; `P5-3b` onwards and `P5-8a` onwards can start. Office hours as a school setting are **not** needed for enforcement now; the System Admin audit view is.
- The guardrail text is changed; the golden hallucination set must not contain a question that still says messaging is limited to office hours (checked when the messaging module is built).
- Messages are children-related personal data: the children's data plan (`documentation/modules/children-data-protection.md`) applies (no child details in push text, retention, erasure).

## Status of the work
- **P5-3b (server for one-to-one conversations): built.** `Conversation` (one per student, teacher and parent account), `Message` (a `clientId` so a repeat is one message; hidden messages keep their words in the audit trail), `ConversationRead`, `MessageReport` (Data Connect; needs the owner's production deploy). `/v1/messages`: `contacts` (a parent sees the teachers of the child's class, a teacher sees the parents of a student in a class she teaches), `threads` (list, with unread), start, open, send, `read`, hide, report. rbac: Teacher and Parent hold `messages` read, create and update at own scope. Messages are delivered at once at any hour (no office-hours hold); each send is audited with its words; the other person gets a notice that names nobody and nothing (category `message`, can be muted); a report notifies the Principal. A sender hides a message for 5 minutes by the gateway's clock. Verified on the emulators: 5 new integration tests, the audit binding file with 4 new samples, notifications, the four matrix parts, student leave and resource comments (7,114 tests).
- **P5-3c (class broadcast, server): built.** `Broadcast` and `BroadcastRecipient` (Data Connect; need the owner's production deploy), `/v1/broadcasts` (send, list, read one, mark read), rbac resource `broadcasts` (Teacher own: send for the class she is class teacher of; Academic admin and Principal school: send to any class; Parent linked: read and mark read). The recipients are worked out **on the server** from the students placed in the class now and their linked parent accounts, written down at send time (a child who moves later neither gains nor loses an old broadcast); a parent with two children in the class is reached once and gets one notice (category `message`, no names, no words). The sender sees `stats`: students in the class, students reached, **the students with no parent account**, parent accounts reached and how many opened it. A repeat of the same `clientId` is one broadcast. Every send is audited with its words. Parents cannot reply into it (a private reply is a conversation). Verified on the emulators: 3 new integration tests, the audit file (sample added), notifications, the four matrix parts, messaging, me (7,219 tests). The locked rbac cell for the Principal's writes now also allows `broadcasts:create` (ADR-0018 decision 3).
- **P5-3d (Teacher and Parent screens): built.** Teacher app: Messages (conversations, New message by student, the conversation with Hide on your own message and Report on theirs) and Class messages ("Send to the whole class" for the classes she is class teacher of, and her sent ones with who was reached and who must be called). Parent app (in More): Messages and Class messages (opening one tells the school; "Reply privately" starts a conversation about the child). Writing a message or a class message and opening a conversation are saved on the phone and sent once (`message.send`, `broadcast.send`, `thread.read`, `broadcast.read`); starting a conversation, hide and report need a connection and say so. Bug found while building the client: the broadcast and grades DTOs were both named `StudentRefDto`, so the generated client expected an `enrollmentId` the broadcast does not carry; the broadcast one is now `BroadcastStudentDto`. Verified on fake gateways only (9 new Dart tests); not run against the real gateway.
- **P5-3e (reviewing messages): built in part.** `GET /v1/messages/reports`, `GET /v1/messages/reports/:id` (the reported words, shown even if the sender hid them, with the whole conversation, the last 200 messages) and `POST /v1/messages/reports/:id/review` for the **Principal** (new rbac resource `message_reports`: read, update; the locked Principal-writes test now names it); `GET /v1/audit/messages` for the **System Admin** (`audit_log:read`): every conversation start, message, hide, report, report closing and class message with its words, who wrote to whom and about which student, newest first, paged. **Opening the trail is itself written down** (`audit.messages.read`, written before the page is returned; refused reads write nothing). Nothing edits or deletes a message. Web-admin: "Reported messages" (Principal) and the Audit log screen (System Admin, messages only for now). Data Connect: `ReviewMessageReport` (Principal only, once), `RecordMessageAuditRead` (System Admin only).
- **P5-8a (notice board, server): built.** `Announcement` gains `audience` (STAFF, ALL_PARENTS, GRADE, CLASS), `category` (General, Holiday, Exam, Event, Fees, Transport), `pinned`, `publishAt`, `expiresAt`, `gradeLevel`, `classroom` (existing rows default to STAFF and General; the staff announcements routes now show STAFF rows only). `/v1/notice-board` (list, read one, `reach` count before posting, post, edit, withdraw), rbac resource `notice_board` (Principal and Academic admin: any category; Bursar: `own`, narrowed to Fees; transport manager: `own`, narrowed to Transport; Parent: `linked`, read). A parent sees only live posts meant for them (all parents, or the grade or class of a linked child); scheduled, expired and withdrawn posts are not shown to parents (staff see all with a state). Parents are told by a notice (`notice.posted`, category `announcement`, the text is the headline), one each, written one by one after the post, best effort. Verified on the emulators: 5 new integration tests; the full gateway run.
- **P5-8a deviations:** (1) a post scheduled for later **tells nobody** (there is no scheduler); parents see it at its time but are not notified. (2) Phones should replace the whole list on each pull (there is no `updatedSince` for parents: a post becoming visible by the clock would be missed by an incremental pull). (3) The category rule for the Bursar and the transport manager is enforced in the gateway; the database checks only that the poster is one of the four roles. (4) No push beyond the in-app notice (real push is P4-6).
- **Not built from P5-3e:** the **Staff app Messages for office roles** (a parent writing to the Bursar about a fee, the Principal, and so on). It needs a decision first: which office roles a parent may start a conversation with, and about what. Office hours as a setting is no longer needed (decision 2).
- **Deviations:** (1) the recipient rows and notices are written one at a time after the broadcast, a few at a time, best effort (a database operation cannot loop); a recipient that fails shows as not reached. (2) **A broadcast to a whole grade is not built**: the Principal and the Academic admin send to one class at a time. (3) "No device" is not part of "not reached" yet (real push does not exist).
- **Not built:** the Staff app Messages for office roles (see P5-3e above).
- **Not verified:** `test:dataconnect` (CI runs it); a phone; Hindi and Urdu notice wording (drafts).
