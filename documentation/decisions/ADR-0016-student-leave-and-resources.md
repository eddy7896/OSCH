# ADR-0016: Student leave from the Parent app, and teachers' learning resources

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-30), owner decisions in chat; three small points were not answered and take the stated default (marked **default**).
- **Plan:** `documentation/modules/student-leave-and-resources.md`. Tasks P5-10 and P5-11 in `documentation/phase5_plan.md`.
- **Builds on:** ADR-0001 (tenant isolation), ADR-0007 (roles; the Parent is not an employee), ADR-0013 (notices written in their cause's transaction), ADR-0014 (offline sync rules, announcements).

## Decisions
1. **A parent may ask leave for their own child**, from the Parent app, with or without a connection (a `clientId`; sent once). The Parent role gains one new resource, `student_leave`, with `linked` scope: read, create and update (update is only cancelling a request that is still waiting). A parent is never an employee and gets no staff leave.
2. **Either the class teacher of the child's class or the Academic admin decides** (`student_leave` update: Teacher at `own` scope, Academic admin at school scope). The first decision stands; the same decision repeated is the same answer; the opposite decision after it is refused (409). The Principal reads. HR, Bursar and the other roles have no access.
3. **Approval is shown, not forced, in attendance.** For a date inside an approved leave, a class's attendance roster starts that child as **Leave** (note "Approved leave") for as long as the day has not been taken. A day that was taken or locked is never changed by a leave request (the Academic admin's audited correction route exists for those). Declining or cancelling writes nothing to attendance. This avoids writing marks into days that do not exist yet.
4. **Notices:** the class teacher is told of a new request, and the parent of the decision, in the in-app inbox in their own language. Until now a notice could only be addressed to a staff member or to a role, so a parent could not be told anything; a notice may now be addressed to **one signed-in person** (`recipientUser`), and the push service sends to that person's phones. The new category is `studentleave` (it can be switched off like the others).
5. **Learning resources** (P5-11): teachers upload files (PDF, JPG, PNG, Word, PowerPoint; at most 20 MB) for a class and subject they teach; every parent of that class this year can read them in the Parent app; a resource is kept **until the end of the school year**, then it leaves the phones and stays in the audit trail. The files live in the private bucket under `schools/<school>/resources/...`; access is by short-lived addresses issued by the gateway; type and size are checked before an address is given and again on the stored file.
6. **Comments under a resource** (P5-11d): parents of that class, the uploading teacher and the Academic admin read and write; text only, 500 characters. A parent's name is shown as "first name, relationship of child's first name" (for example "Parvati, mother of Asha"): **the owner accepted that other parents of the class see a child's first name**. This does not contradict the messaging plan's "no private chat between parents": comments are in the open, in the class, under a school resource. The teacher or the Academic admin can hide a comment (shown as "Hidden by the school"; the author is told); a parent may edit or delete their own comment for 5 minutes after posting; the first version and every change stay in the audit trail; the server's clock decides the window. The teacher and everyone who has commented are told of a new comment.
7. **Defaults taken without an answer (the owner may change them):** no malware scanning of uploads in version 1 (only parents of that class can download); the teacher's notice of a new comment waits for office hours, as the messaging plan proposes; no Report button for parents (hiding is the teacher's or the admin's).

## Amendment (2026-10-31, children's data plan)
Decision 6's display of a commenter changes: a comment shows **"Parent of {child's first name}"** and not the parent's own name, so other families learn only a first name (owner's answer, `documentation/modules/children-data-protection.md` section 1a). Notices pushed to a phone carry no child detail. Not built yet (comments are P5-11d); the text above is superseded where it differs.

## Consequences
- The Data Connect schema gains `StudentLeave` and a nullable `Notification.recipientUser`; both need the owner's deploy to production.
- Teachers and parents of one class become able to read each other's first names in comments; the Parent app must say so (a short line under the comment box) and the privacy policy (P6-5) must mention it.
- The class teacher's list of requests and the Academic admin's page read the same routes at different scopes.
