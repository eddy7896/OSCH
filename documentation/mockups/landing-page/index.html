One system, five kinds of people.

    Principals

    See the whole school in one place: attendance, fees, leave and reports. Approve what needs a decision. The office keeps its own screens.

Children's data

Four promises about your school's data.

A school holds a child's name, marks and whereabouts. Each promise below is built into the system, not only written in a policy.

Promise 01

Your school's data stays yours and separate.

Every school's records are kept apart. A login can only ever reach its own school.

Built in
Promise 02

Parents agree first.

A parent sees nothing about a child until they have read the privacy notice and agreed. They can withdraw at any time.

Built in
Promise 03

No ads, no trackers.

The apps carry no advertising or analytics tools, and alerts on a phone never name a child.

Built in
Promise 04

Every change is recorded.

Who changed an attendance mark, a fee or a grade, and when. Corrections are added, never overwritten.
