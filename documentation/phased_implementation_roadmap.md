# OruSchools Phased Implementation Roadmap

Building a multi-tenant ERP with an offline-first mobile ecosystem requires a strict dependency-based rollout strategy. We must build the "System of Record" (The Web ERP) before we build the "System of Engagement" (The Mobile Apps).

This document outlines the strategic order in which we will build the applications and their respective modules.

---

## Phase 1: The SaaS Foundation & Core Identity
*You cannot onboard a school without a multi-tenant provider.*

- **Target Apps:** `apps/saas-admin`, `apps/api-gateway`, Firebase Data Connect
- **Modules to Build:**
  - **Global Auth & Identity:** Firebase Auth integration, global user mapping (`SUPER_ADMIN` vs `USER`).
  - **Tenant Provisioning:** The SaaS Owner dashboard to create new `School` entities (schemas) and manage subscriptions.
  - **API Gateway Guard:** NestJS middleware to enforce multi-tenant boundaries (ensuring School A cannot query School B's data).

---

## Phase 2: School Web ERP - Core Operations & Finance
*A school needs to be configured, populated, and funded before classes begin.*

- **Target Apps:** `apps/web-admin`, `apps/api-gateway`
- **Modules to Build:**
  - **School Configuration:** Setting up academic years, grades, and classrooms.
  - **People Management (HR/Admissions):** Onboarding Staff (Teachers, Drivers, Admins) and admitting Students.
  - **Financial Ledgers:** Fee generation, invoicing, and basic payroll setup. (Critical path for school operations).

---

## Phase 3: The Academic Engine
*Before teachers can teach, the system must know where they belong.*

- **Target Apps:** `apps/web-admin`
- **Modules to Build:**
  - **Master Scheduling:** Complex timetable generation (mapping Teachers to Classes/Subjects).
  - **Exam Configuration:** Defining grading scales and assessment periods.
  - **Leave Substitution Matrix:** The HR module that automatically assigns substitute teachers based on the Master Schedule.

---

## Phase 4: Teacher & Staff Mobile Ecosystem (Mobile Phase 1)
*Equipping the staff for day-to-day offline-first operations.*

- **Target Apps:** `apps/teacher-app` (Flutter), `apps/staff-app` (Flutter)
- **Modules to Build:**
  - **Background Sync Queue:** Implementing the SQLite/Isar local database and the offline mutation queue that talks to the NestJS API.
  - **Teacher Hub:** Geo-fenced attendance marking, offline gradebook entry.
  - **Staff Utilities:** Leave request submission (which triggers the Phase 3 Substitution Matrix).

---

## Phase 5: Parent & Transport Ecosystem (Mobile Phase 2)
*Parents consume the data generated by Teachers and the Finance team.*

- **Target Apps:** `apps/parent-app` (Flutter), `apps/driver-app` (Flutter)
- **Modules to Build:**
  - **Parent Portal:** Read-only views of attendance and grades.
  - **Fee Payments:** Payment gateway integrations (Stripe/Razorpay) to pay the invoices generated in Phase 2.
  - **Transport & Communication:** Live Bus Tracking (Driver app GPS broadcasting) and 1:1 Direct Messaging (with Teacher Office Hours enforced).

---

## Phase 6: Public Marketing
*Selling the platform.*

- **Target Apps:** `apps/landing-page`
- **Modules to Build:**
  - SEO-optimized marketing pages, feature showcases, and SaaS pricing tiers leading into the `saas-admin` signup flow.
